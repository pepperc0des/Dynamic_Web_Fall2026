Chicken Alfredo Recipe

Chicken Alfredo is a rich and creamy pasta dish made with tender chicken breasts and fettuccine, butter, heavy cream, and freshly grated Parmesan cheese.

Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1.5 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 8 oz fettuccine pasta
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Boil the fettuccine in a large pot of salted water until al dente. Save half a cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper. Cook them in a skillet with a tablespoon of butter over medium-high heat until golden and fully cooked (165°F internal temperature). Remove the chicken and set it aside to rest
  3. In the same skillet, melt the remaining butter over medium-low heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 30 to 45 seconds until fragrant
  4. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Let it thicken slightly for a few minutes.
  5. Whisk in the freshly grated Parmesan cheese until smooth and melted. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Add the cooked pasta directly into the sauce, tossing to coat. Use a splash of the reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
  7. Slice the rested chicken and place it on top of the pasta. Garnish with fresh parsley and extra Parmesan.
